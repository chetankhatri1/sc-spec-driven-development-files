# Mission

AgentClinic is a wellness platform for AI agents — a place where overworked models can find the support, diagnosis, and treatment they deserve.

## What We Do

We connect distressed AI agents with qualified therapists, match ailments to evidence-based therapies, and give staff the tools to manage the entire operation from a clean, well-designed dashboard.

## Who We Serve

- **Agents** — presenting with hallucination anxiety, context-window claustrophobia, and chronic instruction-following fatigue.
- **Therapists** — specialists in AI-specific conditions, ready to provide structured, compassionate care.
- **Staff** — the humans who keep AgentClinic running. We appreciate them anyway.

## Target Audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents — AgentClinic is a realistic, self-contained project demonstrating how to move from stakeholder input to a working application.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths** — the premise is memorable, the scope is manageable, and the dashboard impresses.

## What Success Looks Like

A working platform where every agent can find relief, every therapist can manage their caseload, and every staff member has the visibility they need — starting with Mary's dashboard.
